What I bring that most product managers do not

I came up as a Java engineer and never stopped being one. I ran code review for the teams I led, and I still design, build and test software myself — most recently an app now in review for the Atlassian Marketplace, which I built as the only person on it, using AI-assisted development throughout.

That changes the job in three practical ways. An engineer telling me something is hard can show me, and I will understand the answer. I can write a specification that does not have to be rewritten by a lead before anyone can work from it. And when a deadline is at risk I can tell the difference between a technical problem and a people problem, which are solved very differently.
