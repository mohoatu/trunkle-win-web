What I am doing now

Table Audit & Export for Confluence — an Atlassian Forge app I designed, built and tested on my own, submitted to the Atlassian Marketplace.

It reads every table on a Confluence page and checks the arithmetic inside invoice and order tables: quantity times unit price against the line total, the lines against the total row, subtotal plus tax minus discount against the stated total, and a tax line against its own stated rate. It also flags dates and numbers that two spreadsheets would read differently — "04/02/2025", "1,410" — and exports a clean .xlsx with real dates and real numbers.

It runs entirely in the browser, holds Atlassian's Runs on Atlassian badge, and contains no AI: the promise to users is that their table never leaves their machine, and arithmetic does not need a model.

Covered by 724 automated checks and a mutation suite of 125 deliberate breakages, each of which the tests must catch. Three languages of column headings: English, Vietnamese, German.
