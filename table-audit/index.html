Table Audit & Export for Confluence

An Atlassian Forge app by Le Hoang Quang Trung

← trunkle.win Documentation Support Security Security review Privacy

Finds data problems in Confluence tables and exports them to Excel.

It reads every table on a Confluence page and checks the arithmetic inside invoice and order tables: quantity times unit price against the line total, the lines against the total row, subtotal plus tax minus discount against the stated total, and a tax line against its own stated rate. It also flags dates and numbers that two spreadsheets would read differently — 04/02/2025, 1,410 — and exports a clean .xlsx with real dates and real numbers.

Column headings are recognised in three languages: English, Vietnamese and German.

How it is built

  • Runs entirely in the browser. Qualifies for Atlassian's Runs on Atlassian programme: no customer data leaves Atlassian infrastructure.
  • No AI in the product, by deliberate choice. The promise is that a customer's table never leaves their machine — and arithmetic does not need a model.
  • 743 automated checks, plus a mutation suite of 125 deliberate breakages the tests must catch. A suite that can never go red makes "all green" meaningless.
  • I publish a security review of my own app, listing the defects I found in my own work.
