PRIVACY POLICY — Table Audit & Export for Confluence

Last updated: 2026-09-28

WHAT THIS APP DOES WITH YOUR DATA

Table Audit & Export reads the tables on a Confluence page you choose, checks them for data problems, and can build a spreadsheet file from them.

All of this happens inside your own browser.

WE DO NOT COLLECT ANY DATA

We operate no server and no database for this app. Specifically:

• We do not receive, store, transmit or have access to the content of your pages or tables at any time.
• We do not collect analytics, usage statistics, telemetry or error reports.
• We do not use cookies or any tracking technology.
• We do not create user accounts.
• Nothing is shared with any third party, because we hold nothing to share.

The spreadsheet file is built by code running on your own machine and saved directly by your browser. It is never uploaded anywhere.

PERMISSIONS

The app requests one permission: read access to Confluence pages (read:page:confluence). It cannot modify your pages and cannot access spaces, users, attachments or any other Confluence data. This limit is enforced by the Atlassian Forge platform, not by us — requests outside the granted permission are rejected by Atlassian before they reach any data.

WHERE THE APP RUNS

The app is a Forge app. Its static files are served by Atlassian's own infrastructure. When the app reads a page, the request goes from your browser to Atlassian's API and the response returns to your browser. No part of that path involves us.

CHILDREN

The app is a workplace tool and is not directed at children.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
