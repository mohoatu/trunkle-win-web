SUPPORT — Table Audit & Export for Confluence

Looking for setup steps and how the app works? See the documentation.

How to get help
Email support@trunkle.win. We reply within two business days.

When reporting a problem, please include:
• What you clicked and what happened
• A screenshot of the app window if it showed an error
• Roughly how many rows and columns the table has

Please do not send us the contents of your table. We do not need it, and we would rather not receive it.

Known limits
• The app reads tables on a single page at a time.
• Tables inside an expand are read; the app tells you when a table came from one.
• Values that could be read more than one way are exported as text rather than converted, on purpose.
